Heat Meds

Some everyday medicines make hot days more dangerous.

Type in your medicines and ZIP code. Get one printable page that says which of your medicines matter in heat, what this week's heat looks like where you live, and what to ask your pharmacist.

Try an example Mom, 74, in Phoenix: a water pill, a blood pressure pill, an antidepressant, insulin, and a cholesterol pill.

Type each name as it appears on the bottle, brand or generic, then press Add.

    Add at least one medicine to see a plan.

    Without a ZIP you still get the medicine check, just not this week's heat.

    Heat forecast: National Weather Service HeatRisk (experimental). ZIP locations: U.S. Census Bureau ZCTA Gazetteer. Medicine names and classes: NLM RxNorm and RxClass.
